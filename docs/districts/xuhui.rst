Before applying
---------------

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Read the dated call and verify district, entity, activity and spending-period rules.

#. Prepare the prescribed pack and complete host or district endorsement if required.

#. Submit through the official platform before the stated deadline and keep the receipt.

#. Follow review, award, reporting, acceptance and payment obligations in the notice.

Ask which current notice governs the project, whether registration and actual operations must both be in the district, which expenditure period is eligible, and whether previous municipal or district awards exclude a claim. Obtain the answer before moving a company or incurring costs.
