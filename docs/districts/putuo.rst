Putuo
============

Industrial testing, intelligent software and international innovation

Wuning Science Park connects industrial research, testing and technology transfer. The district incubator register also lists the China-Israel Innovation Hub; tenancy and investment require separate terms.

`Evidence S013 <https://www.shpt.gov.cn/ys-cyyq/20260430/969703.html>`_. 上海武宁科技园_创业园区_上海市普陀区人民政府.

Who receives the inquiry
------------------------

Putuo science and technology authority

`Official district website <https://www.shpt.gov.cn/>`_

Use the official website to request the responsible team and current application route. Email addresses are not reproduced in this publication.

Before applying
---------------

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Read the dated call and verify district, entity, activity and spending-period rules.

#. Prepare the prescribed pack and complete host or district endorsement if required.

#. Submit through the official platform before the stated deadline and keep the receipt.

#. Follow review, award, reporting, acceptance and payment obligations in the notice.

Ask which current notice governs the project, whether registration and actual operations must both be in the district, which expenditure period is eligible, and whether previous municipal or district awards exclude a claim. Obtain the answer before moving a company or incurring costs.

Local organizations in this atlas
---------------------------------

* :doc:`Wuning Science Park </entities/wuning>` (上海武宁科技园 · Technology park · Hardware AI, Industry) — An industrial research and testing environment operated by the Shanghai Electrical Apparatus Research Institute group. Robot and industrial-internet testing are useful entry points for hardware validation. [Status: Published entity; intake to confirm]
* :doc:`China-Israel Innovation Hub, Shanghai </entities/china-israel>` (中以（上海）创新园 · Technology park · Software AI, Hardware AI) — A district-listed carrier for international collaboration in intelligent manufacturing, AI, robotics and information technology. It supports market, capital and industrial connections; the listing does not guarantee cash support. [Status: Published entity; intake to confirm]
* :doc:`Shanghai Tsinghua International Innovation Center </entities/tsinghua-shanghai>` (上海清华国际创新中心 · Research platform · Software AI, Hardware AI, Chips) — A strategic research and technology-transfer platform in Putuo's "One Axis, One Park, One Center" layout, connecting university engineering laboratories with Yangtze River Delta industrial applications. [Status: Published entity; intake to confirm]
* :doc:`East China Normal University </entities/university-ecnu>` (华东师范大学 · University) — Institutional entry in the June 2026 Ministry of Education register. Suggested collaboration themes: Software, education AI, data science, microelectronics. These themes are editorial routing guidance, not a verified offer of laboratory access or startup funding. [Status: Published entity; intake to confirm]
