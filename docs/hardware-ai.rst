Intelligence in the physical world
==================================

Hardware joins algorithms to materials, manufacturing tolerances, reliability and service. A beautiful prototype is the beginning of a supply chain. We follow chips, sensors, robots and autonomous systems through the facilities they need.

Chips and sensing
-----------------

For an AI chip project, prepare the architecture, workload, process choice, toolchain, IP dependencies and software support plan. Separate EDA access, licensed IP, multi-project wafer participation, full mask sets, packaging, testing and characterization. A shared lab or incubator does not automatically pay for a tape-out.

Zhangjiang IC Design Industrial Park and the IC promotion center are initial service routes. In Jiading, the documented SITRI and InnoSpring relationship is relevant to beyond-Moore devices, sensors and pilot production. We ask for equipment, process capability, queue time, sample ownership and a written quotation.

Robots and embodied AI
----------------------

Separate the robot foundation model, perception, planning, control and safety architecture from motors, transmissions, tactile sensing, batteries and the mechanical platform. Test the complete system against a named use case. Demonstrations should state payload, cycle time, failure conditions, human intervention and maintenance cost.

Compare Minhang's research-to-manufacturing connection, Baoshan's robot industry, Wuning's testing services and Zhangjiang's robotics ecosystem. Corporate programs can provide a deployment conversation. They do not replace production qualification, applicable certification or a customer acceptance plan.

Other physical frontiers
------------------------

For drones, begin with a real operating scenario and ask Jinshan's UAV route about test services and applicable operating permissions. For brain-computer interfaces and medical devices, separate exploratory research from regulated product development. For AI-enabled biology, confirm biosafety, instrument availability, sample handling and clinical access before renting a lab.

Our hardware application pack adds a bill of materials, costed engineering milestones, manufacturing partner plan, test fixtures, reliability plan and a working demonstration. We identify what the incubator can actually supply and what the team must buy elsewhere.
