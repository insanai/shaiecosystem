Zhangjiang Jinshan Park low-altitude UAV and new materials support
==================================================================

Grant / reimbursement | Jinshan

Policy in force; call not confirmed open

Amount and mechanism
--------------------

Policy-specific R&D, carrier incubation, testing-service and industrialization support under the 2025-2026 park action plan

No equity

Eligibility and timing
----------------------

Qualifying enterprises and innovation carriers in Zhangjiang Jinshan Park working on low-altitude UAV systems, new chemical or electronic materials, biomanufacturing and intelligent equipment.

Policy: 2025 to 2026 action plan; confirm current implementing batch
