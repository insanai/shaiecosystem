Fund announced; deal intake to confirm

Amount and mechanism
--------------------

Fund target RMB 2bn; first tranche RMB 500m; individual cheque not established

Negotiated equity

Eligibility and timing
----------------------

Seed-stage AI projects fitting the manager's mandate. The public launch does not establish a universal application form, valuation or shareholding percentage.

Manager-led sourcing and due diligence

Contact and application
-----------------------

`Official application / policy route <https://www.pudong.gov.cn/>`_

The linked authority or operator is the contact route. A direct current program contact is not established in this profile.

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Confirm the manager, sector, stage and legal-entity fit.

#. Submit a deck, cap table, technology evidence, traction and financing plan.

#. Complete commercial, financial, technical and legal due diligence.

#. Review term sheet, investment committee conditions and definitive agreements.

The published maximum is a ceiling, not an entitlement. Check the applicable notice, eligible cost period, other public support and written payment conditions.
