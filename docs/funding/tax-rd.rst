R&D super-deduction
===================

Tax relief | Citywide / global

Published tax guidance; applicability review

`Evidence S099 <https://shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/xwdt/ztzl/zcgll/jsjf/rdwd/202602/t479502.html>`_. 上海市“2366”产业税收优惠政策指引.

Amount and mechanism
--------------------

Eligible expensed R&D: additional 100% deduction; eligible capitalized R&D: 200% amortization basis

No equity

Eligibility and timing
----------------------

Qualifying enterprise R&D costs with the required accounting and evidence. A tax deduction reduces taxable income; it is not cash equal to the expense or deduction.

Annual tax filing and applicable rules

Contact and application
-----------------------

`Official application / policy route <https://shanghai.chinatax.gov.cn/>`_

The linked authority or operator is the contact route. A direct current program contact is not established in this profile.

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Check the current tax rules and recognition or accounting requirements.

#. Maintain qualifying R&D and income records with a qualified tax adviser.

#. Complete recognition where required and claim in the correct tax filing.

#. Retain evidence for review and separate tax savings from cash grants.

The published maximum is a ceiling, not an entitlement. Check the applicable notice, eligible cost period, other public support and written payment conditions.
