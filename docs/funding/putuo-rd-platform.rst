Putuo R&D Platform Projects, 2026
=================================

Grant | Putuo

2026 call published; not confirmed open

`Evidence S086 <https://www.shpt.gov.cn/zhengwu/kjptjs-kwkjcxnlts/2026/244/205993.html>`_. 关于开展2026年度普陀区科技研发平台项目申报工作的通知-上海普陀.

Amount and mechanism
--------------------

Up to RMB 2 million; first payment at most 50%, balance subject to acceptance

No equity

Eligibility and timing
----------------------

Lawfully established, good-credit legal or non-legal-person organizations building qualifying R&D platforms. Online registration requires district review; credit commitments and prescribed materials are required.

Confirm the deadline in the September 2026 notice

Contact and application
-----------------------

`Official application / policy route <https://kwxxh.shpt.gov.cn/v2/ptoa/#/user/register>`_

Published consultation: +86 21 52564588 ext. 8628.

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Read the dated call and verify district, entity, activity and spending-period rules.

#. Prepare the prescribed pack and complete host or district endorsement if required.

#. Submit through the official platform before the stated deadline and keep the receipt.

#. Follow review, award, reporting, acceptance and payment obligations in the notice.

The published maximum is a ceiling, not an entitlement. Check the applicable notice, eligible cost period, other public support and written payment conditions.
