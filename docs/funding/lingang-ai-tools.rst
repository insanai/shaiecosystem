Lin-gang OPC AI-tool service cost support
=========================================

Reimbursement | Pudong

Published policy; implementing intake to confirm

`Evidence S139 <https://lingang.gov.cn/html/website/lg/English/Policies/Texts/2039941467013750785.html>`_. Lin-gang OPC policy: AI tool services and open-source development, 2026.

Amount and mechanism
--------------------

Up to 80% of qualifying AI-tool service costs; applicable caps and conditions require the implementing notice

No equity for qualifying public support

Eligibility and timing
----------------------

Eligible OPCs purchasing approved technical services under the March 2026 measures. Foreign-founded teams should confirm the required local entity, community recognition, eligible invoices and supplier rules. Foreign ownership or a foreign parent alone does not establish grant eligibility; verify the written current procedure.

Confirm current implementing notice and intake

Contact and application
-----------------------

`Official application / policy route <https://p.lingang.gov.cn/>`_

The linked authority or operator is the contact route. A direct current program contact is not established in this profile.

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Confirm the actual category, area boundary, supplier and eligible expenditure period.

#. Keep service contracts, invoices, payment evidence and itemized usage logs.

#. Use the named platform and current batch; obtain the required district or operator review.

#. Check approved amount, duplicate-funding exclusions and payment conditions.

The published maximum is a ceiling, not an entitlement. Check the applicable notice, eligible cost period, other public support and written payment conditions.
