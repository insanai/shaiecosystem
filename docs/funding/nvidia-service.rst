NVIDIA Inception
================

In-kind services | Citywide / global

Program published; eligibility review

`Evidence S040 <https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/>`_. Inception Program for Startups | NVIDIA.

Amount and mechanism
--------------------

Free membership; conditional ecosystem benefits, not a cash grant

No equity

Eligibility and timing
----------------------

Qualifying AI startups; check incorporation, developer, age and regional conditions. Product availability and partner credits are separately governed.

Online application; regional eligibility review

Contact and application
-----------------------

`Official application / policy route <https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/startups/>`_

The linked authority or operator is the contact route. A direct current program contact is not established in this profile.

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Check incorporation, company age, account region and existing benefits.

#. Apply through the official program form or qualifying partner route.

#. Confirm covered services, expiry, fees, usage limits and overage billing.

#. Keep usage records and do not describe credits as unrestricted cash.

The published maximum is a ceiling, not an entitlement. Check the applicable notice, eligible cost period, other public support and written payment conditions.
