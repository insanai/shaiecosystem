Fengxian R&D expenditure increase reimbursement, 2026
=====================================================

Reimbursement | Fengxian

Closed

`Evidence S125 <https://www.fengxian.gov.cn/kw/tzgg/20260602/108031.html>`_. 奉贤区2026年“南上海科创中心”创新行动计划——研发费用补贴项目申报指南_通知公告.

Amount and mechanism
--------------------

30% of the qualifying annual R&D net increase, capped at RMB 1 million

No equity

Eligibility and timing
----------------------

Valid recognized high-tech enterprise or 2025 registered technology SME; 2024 and 2025 tax finalization and R&D super-deduction schedules are required. Submit online and route the paper pack through the recommending unit.

Online closed 17 June 2026, 17:00; paper closed 30 June 2026, 17:00 China Standard Time
