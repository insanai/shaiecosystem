Minhang core technology R&D projects
====================================

Grant | Minhang

Policy in force; call not confirmed open

`Evidence S101 <https://english.shanghai.gov.cn/en-PolicyInsights/20250919/38bbef698b904adabd605170e7720a4d.html>`_. Minhang amplifies enterprise sci-tech innovation_Policy Insights.

Amount and mechanism
--------------------

General: up to RMB 3m at up to 30% of R&D; major: up to RMB 10m at up to 50%

No equity

Eligibility and timing
----------------------

Project classification and investment thresholds apply. This is selective R&D support, not a general startup incorporation bonus.

Policy through 31 Aug 2030

Contact and application
-----------------------

`Official application / policy route <https://www.shmh.gov.cn/>`_

The linked authority or operator is the contact route. A direct current program contact is not established in this profile.

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Read the dated call and verify district, entity, activity and spending-period rules.

#. Prepare the prescribed pack and complete host or district endorsement if required.

#. Submit through the official platform before the stated deadline and keep the receipt.

#. Follow review, award, reporting, acceptance and payment obligations in the notice.

The published maximum is a ceiling, not an entitlement. Check the applicable notice, eligible cost period, other public support and written payment conditions.
