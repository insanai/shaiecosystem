Jing'an Service Industry Guidance Fund and AI Demonstration Support, 2026
=========================================================================

Grant | Jing'an

2026 call published; deadline check required

`Evidence S130 <https://www.jingan.gov.cn/govxxgk/JA2/2026-05-18/117b6400-28b1-4035-8fc1-3e28685f8668.html>`_. 关于开展2026年第二批静安区服务业发展引导资金项目申报工作的通知.

Amount and mechanism
--------------------

Project-specific support under the district service-industry guidance fund rules, matched to qualifying project investment

No equity

Eligibility and timing
----------------------

Lawfully registered, tax-paying legal-person organizations in Jing'an with sound credit, advancing modern services, enterprise AI, data services or digital commerce under the 2026 batch rules and 2026-2028 AI demonstration plan.

Second 2026 batch published May 2026; confirm current sub-district recommending window

Contact and application
-----------------------

`Official application / policy route <https://www.jingan.gov.cn/govxxgk/JA2/2026-05-18/117b6400-28b1-4035-8fc1-3e28685f8668.html>`_

The linked authority or operator is the contact route. A direct current program contact is not established in this profile.

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Read the dated call and verify district, entity, activity and spending-period rules.

#. Prepare the prescribed pack and complete host or district endorsement if required.

#. Submit through the official platform before the stated deadline and keep the receipt.

#. Follow review, award, reporting, acceptance and payment obligations in the notice.

The published maximum is a ceiling, not an entitlement. Check the applicable notice, eligible cost period, other public support and written payment conditions.
