Zhangjiang AI Town compute, model and corpus support
====================================================

Reimbursement | Pudong

Quarterly scheme published; current intake to confirm

`Evidence S103 <https://www.pudong.gov.cn/zwgk/qsjj_zdgz_lhbl/2026/156/355831.html>`_. 浦数局办理〔2026〕16号（对浦东新区七届人大六次会议第076146号代表建议的答复）_两会办理.

Amount and mechanism
--------------------

Up to 100% of approved actual cost, capped at RMB 1m in each category; at most one year

No equity

Eligibility and timing
----------------------

AI companies registered and operating inside the specified town boundary, established or moved there no earlier than 1 Jan 2025. Expenses must be for qualifying model R&D or deployment; review the current quarterly guide.

Quarterly claims; confirm the current batch

Contact and application
-----------------------

`Official application / policy route <https://pyd.pudong.gov.cn/>`_

Published consultation: +86 21 58811024 ext. 5091.

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Confirm the actual category, area boundary, supplier and eligible expenditure period.

#. Keep service contracts, invoices, payment evidence and itemized usage logs.

#. Use the named platform and current batch; obtain the required district or operator review.

#. Check approved amount, duplicate-funding exclusions and payment conditions.

The published maximum is a ceiling, not an entitlement. Check the applicable notice, eligible cost period, other public support and written payment conditions.
