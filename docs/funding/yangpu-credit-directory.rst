Contact and application
-----------------------

`Official application / policy route <https://xyd.shyp.gov.cn/finance>`_

The linked authority or operator is the contact route. A direct current program contact is not established in this profile.

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Select the current bank or public guarantee product and check borrower eligibility.

#. Prepare accounts, cash-flow forecast, ownership and requested security documents.

#. Complete lender and guarantee assessment; compare all costs and repayment terms.

#. Sign and track debt service; claim interest support only if independently eligible.

The published maximum is a ceiling, not an entitlement. Check the applicable notice, eligible cost period, other public support and written payment conditions.
