Amount and mechanism
--------------------

Competition awards plus priority routing to district startup subsidies, carrier incubation and guaranteed loans

Award / investment terms vary

Eligibility and timing
----------------------

Startup teams and early-stage enterprises aligned with Chongming ecological tech, smart agriculture, marine equipment, green energy and digital economy.

Implementation plan issued 20 August 2026; check track schedule

Contact and application
-----------------------

`Official application / policy route <https://www.shcm.gov.cn/>`_

The linked authority or operator is the contact route. A direct current program contact is not established in this profile.

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Choose the correct competition track, region and applicant category.

#. Read the rules and submit the required project, team and IP evidence.

#. Complete selection or pitching; confirm prize and service conditions in writing.

#. Treat investor introductions and district funding as separate follow-up decisions.

The published maximum is a ceiling, not an entitlement. Check the applicable notice, eligible cost period, other public support and written payment conditions.
