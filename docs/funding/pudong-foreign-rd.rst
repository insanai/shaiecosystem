Pudong foreign-funded R&D and open-innovation support
=====================================================

Grant / market access | Pudong

Published policy; implementing intake to confirm

`Evidence S083 <https://www.pudong.gov.cn/zwgk/14506.gkml_zcwj_gfxwj/2025/105/340086.html>`_. Pudong foreign-investment measures: foreign-funded R&D and open innovation centers.

Amount and mechanism
--------------------

Conditional R&D-center support under the applicable recognition and funding rules; no universal cash ceiling established

No equity for qualifying public support

Eligibility and timing
----------------------

The 2025 measures explicitly support qualifying foreign-funded R&D centers and foreign-invested open innovation centers (GOI). Relevant to AI and hardware companies considering a Pudong or Waigaoqiao research presence. A foreign parent alone is insufficient: confirm local registration, center recognition, project location, sector restrictions and the actual funding call with the Pudong commerce authority.

Policy through 31 December 2027; confirm the implementing call

Contact and application
-----------------------

`Official application / policy route <https://www.pudong.gov.cn/zwgk/14506.gkml_zcwj_gfxwj/2025/105/340086.html>`_

The linked authority or operator is the contact route. A direct current program contact is not established in this profile.

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Read the dated call and verify district, entity, activity and spending-period rules.

#. Prepare the prescribed pack and complete host or district endorsement if required.

#. Submit through the official platform before the stated deadline and keep the receipt.

#. Follow review, award, reporting, acceptance and payment obligations in the notice.

The published maximum is a ceiling, not an entitlement. Check the applicable notice, eligible cost period, other public support and written payment conditions.
