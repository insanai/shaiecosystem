Waigaoqiao bonded R&D and AI-health innovation services
=======================================================

外高桥保税研发与AI健康创新服务

Research platform | Pudong | Research checked 2026-11-04

AI and industry focus: AI for Science, Health, Hardware AI, Robotics.

The 2025-2026 Free Trade Bonded Park plan explicitly supports AI drug discovery, cross-border medical-device R&D, advanced-equipment innovation and foreign-funded R&D institutions. Foreign AI and hardware companies can use the authority route to explore local research, testing and commercialization arrangements. Confirm the specific operator, legal entity, customs boundary, sector permissions and data handling before incurring costs. This is a service and collaboration route; the plan is not a cash-award offer.

Application workflow
--------------------

Editorial application checklist. The current official notice or agreement controls the actual procedure.

#. Find the relevant research group and state the scientific or industrial problem.

#. Send a short proposal covering data, equipment, outputs, funding and timescale.

#. Agree the collaboration route, publication, IP and institutional authorization.

#. Obtain written access or project approval before beginning the work.
