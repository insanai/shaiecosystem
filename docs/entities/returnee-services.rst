Shanghai overseas-returnee entrepreneurship service windows
===========================================================

上海留学人员创业服务窗口

Government | Citywide / global | Research checked 2026-11-04

AI and industry focus: Software AI, Hardware AI.

The 2026 municipal rules identify the talent authority and district service windows for eligible overseas returnees, including qualifying people who acquired foreign citizenship. Recognition and startup-park services can lead to separate support applications. Returnee status is not a category covering every foreign founder and is not an automatic grant or residence permit.
