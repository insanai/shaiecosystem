Shanghai Ecosystem Atlas
========================

The Pearl of the Orient. A field guide to research, enterprise and possibility.

An illustrated guide in seven languages for accelerators, founders, researchers and AI enthusiasts. It connects Shanghai's universities, software and hardware AI communities, and practical routes to support across all 16 districts.

Research edition: 2026-11-04. 131 organization profiles. 50 funding routes. 16 districts. 140 cited sources.

Written by **Vikrant Rathore** with contributions from **Lauren Zhao, Vicky Ni, Jennifer Yuan, Ronak Rathore, Kanak Rathore and Emily Yue Xu**. Produced using **Guidedog** as a unified seven-language **HTML and PDF** publication inspired by Shanghai's official flower, the white magnolia (*Yulania denudata*).

   Complete chapters & directories in this atlas
   ---------------------------------------------

   * :doc:`A city of connected possibilities <reading-the-city>` — Spatial logic across central Shanghai, industrial corridors, five新城 (New Cities) and Chongming Eco-Island, plus magnolia design notes and authorship credits.
   * :doc:`Explore the Shanghai ecosystem <atlas>` — Interactive 16-district vector map, bilingual search, sector and equity filters, shortlist and CSV export.
   * :doc:`Intelligence in software <software-ai>` — Foundation models, vertical agents, Model Speed Space, Zhangjiang Model Community, compute/corpus vouchers and compliance routing.
   * :doc:`Intelligence in the physical world <hardware-ai>` — AI chips, EDA/MPW tape-out, MEMS sensors, embodied robotics, low-altitude drones, BCI and wet-lab biocomputing.
   * :doc:`Sixteen districts, sixteen entry routes <districts>` — Dedicated chapter for each of Shanghai's 16 districts with receiving authorities, policy boundaries, local carriers and funding.
   * :doc:`Universities and commercialization <universities>` — Profiles for all 40 undergraduate universities in Shanghai with MOE codes, collaboration themes and tech-transfer contacts.
   * :doc:`The higher-education register <university-register>` — Complete June 2026 Ministry of Education register: 40 undergraduate universities, 29 diploma colleges, 12 adult institutions and CEIBS.
   * :doc:`Incubators, accelerators and parks <incubation>` — 28 researched incubators, accelerators and technology parks across Shanghai with admission checklists and operator details.
   * :doc:`Research and technical platforms <research>` — Shanghai AI Lab, ICC chip service center and Tongji autonomous and PAI research platforms.
   * :doc:`Corporate and global programs <corporate>` — Roche, SAP.iO, ABB, DroidUp, PaddlePaddle, Yangtze AI+ Center, JLABS, Bayer Co.Lab, NVIDIA Inception, Microsoft, AWS, Siemens and BMW.
   * :doc:`Government entry points <government>` — All 16 district science, technology and economy authorities with official portals, published contacts and policy notes.
   * :doc:`Capital, with its conditions attached <funding-guide>` — How to distinguish non-repayable grants, post-expenditure reimbursements, vouchers, debt, tax relief and equity investment.
   * :doc:`Funding, without mixing the mechanisms <funding>` — All 40 citywide and district funding pathways with ceilings, equity rules, eligibility criteria, deadlines and official links.
   * :doc:`Investors and patient capital <investors>` — STEFG Angel Fund, Shanghai Leading Industry Funds, Pudong AI Seed Fund, Embodied AI Fund, STVC and Qiming Venture Partners.
   * :doc:`From introduction to application <apply>` — Bilingual outreach templates, shared data-room checklist, decision workflows and a four-week accelerator field program.
   * :doc:`How to read the evidence <methodology>` — Source verification rules, translation discipline, map accuracy disclosures, known gaps and authorship credits.
   * :doc:`Sources and reading room <sources>` — Complete bibliography of all cited government notices, institutional registers and operator pages with access dates.
